    Crypto Clarity · Chapter by chapter

    What each chapter covers

    Every chapter ends with key takeaways and three questions to check yourself. Where we have a free article or calculator on the same topic, it is linked so you can try the idea before you buy.

    Part I · Foundations

    1. What Is Crypto, Really?. Starts from the idea that money is a record, not an object, then explains the double-spending problem that kept digital cash unsolved for decades and what changes when software and a network of computers keep the ledger instead of one institution.

    2. How Blockchains Work. How independent computers agree on a single shared ledger without a central operator, and why a record built this way is hard to rewrite after the fact.

    3. Bitcoin: The Original. Where Bitcoin came from, what it was designed to do and where its limits are. Free companion reading: Bitcoin live data and history and the halving explained.

    4. Ethereum, Smart Contracts and the Wider Landscape. What smart contracts are, how Ethereum differs from Bitcoin, and how other networks fit into the picture. Free companion reading: Ethereum and Solana pages.

    Part II · The Ecosystem

    5. Coins, Tokens and Categories. How to tell a coin from a token, how projects are grouped into categories, and why a category label alone tells you little about quality.

    6. Wallets, Keys and Self-Custody. What a private key really is, the difference between leaving crypto on an exchange and holding it yourself, and the trade-offs of each.

    7. Exchanges and How Markets Work. How exchanges match buyers and sellers, what fees and spreads cost you, and how order types behave. Free companion reading: how to read an order book and stop-loss orders explained.

    8. DeFi Explained. Lending, decentralised exchanges and liquidity pools in plain language, with the risks that come with each. Free companion reading: impermanent loss explained and staking APR vs APY.

    Part III · Markets and Analysis

    9. Reading Charts. How to read price charts and timeframes, and what support, resistance and trends can and cannot tell you. Free companion reading: round-number levels.

    10. Indicators and Their Limits. What popular indicators actually calculate, and why no indicator predicts price. Free tool: the Fibonacci retracement calculator.

    11. Fundamental and On-Chain Research. The questions worth asking about a project, and what public blockchain data can and cannot tell you.

    12. Market Cycles, Sentiment and Psychology. How markets move in cycles of fear and greed and how your own emotions work against you. Free companion reading: the Fear & Greed Index explained.

    Part IV · Risk, Safety and Your Plan

    13. Risk Management. Sizing positions, limiting the loss on any one trade and thinking in worst cases before they happen. Free tools: position size and risk-reward calculators, plus the 1% rule.

    14. Spot, Margin and Futures. How spot, margin and futures trading differ, and the arithmetic of leverage and liquidation. Free tools: the liquidation price calculator and spot vs futures.

    15. Scams, Hacks and Security. Common scam patterns, how hacks happen and a security checklist you can apply today.

    16. Taxes, Regulation and Staying Legal. Why the rules differ from country to country, which records to keep and when to ask a professional. Free tool: the average price calculator for tracking cost.

    Most people meet crypto the wrong way round. They see a price chart, a headline about someone who got rich, or a friend’s screenshot, and they feel they must act before the moment passes. Understanding comes later, usually after the first expensive lesson.

Nobody can reliably predict crypto prices, and anyone who says otherwise is selling something. The goal here is different: to make you hard to fool and hard to wipe out, and to leave you able to form your own opinions.

    Candlestick Patterns · Why it is different

    Honest about what patterns can and cannot do

    Most candlestick material hands you a shape and a promise. This book tells you what each shape says about buyers and sellers, then shows where it tends to fail. Several pages carry a research note comparing the textbook claim with measured results from Thomas Bulkowski's large tests of 103 patterns on stock data, and the book explains why those results may differ on crypto.

    You also get a crypto-specific note on every pattern: 24/7 trading means true price gaps are rare, liquidation wicks can fake a hammer, and the same candle means different things on a thin weekend than on a busy Tuesday. The goal is to make you hard to fool, not to promise profit.

    Who is this ebook for?

    Complete beginners and early-stage traders who want to understand how crypto works and how to avoid the common, expensive mistakes. No prior knowledge is assumed.

    Does it tell me what to buy?

    No. It does not recommend assets, give signals or predict prices. It teaches you how to research, size risk and stay safe so that you can decide for yourself.

    Can I read a sample first?

    Yes, for both books. The Crypto Clarity sample has the preface, contents and all of Chapter 1 (16 pages). The Candlestick Patterns sample has the contents, two chapters and five full pattern pages (18 pages).

    Which ebook should I start with?

    If you are new to crypto, start with Crypto Clarity: it explains how blockchains, wallets, exchanges and risk work. If you already trade or follow charts and want to read candles properly, start with Candlestick Patterns. Many readers take both.

    Does the candlestick book promise profits or predict prices?

    No. Patterns describe what buyers and sellers just did; they do not predict the future. The book shows where each pattern fails, includes research notes where results are available (mostly from stock data, not crypto) and teaches position sizing so that one wrong call does not wipe you out.
