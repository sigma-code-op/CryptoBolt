        How each lesson works

        1. Read. Short sections, plain words, examples with simple numbers.
        2. Play. Several lessons have a tool: build a candle, size a trade, find a liquidation price.
        3. Practise. Each lesson ends with a small exercise in the terminal or paper trading.

        What this course will and will not do

        It will

        • Teach you the vocabulary and mechanics so nothing on a trading screen is a mystery.
        • Show you how costs, leverage and position size really affect an account.
        • Give you a plan, a journal and a safe way to practise.

        It will not

        • Give you signals, predictions or a "secret strategy".
        • Promise profits. Most beginners lose money, and that is why risk comes first here.
        • Replace advice from a licensed professional about your own finances.

        Common questions

        Do I get a certificate?

        Yes. Mark all twelve lessons complete and you can download a personal certificate of completion as a PNG or PDF. Score 90% or more across all twelve quizzes for the With Distinction version. It is a record of your study, not an accredited qualification.

        Is the course really free?

        Yes. All twelve lessons, quizzes and tools are free, with no sign-up. Your progress is saved in your own browser, not on our servers.

        Do I need any experience or money?

        No. The course assumes you know nothing about trading. You practise with virtual money in paper trading, so you do not need to deposit anything.

        Will this course make me profitable?

        No course can promise that, and we do not. Most beginners lose money. This course teaches the habits that protect your account while you learn: risk control, planning and record keeping.

        Is this about crypto only?

        The examples and tools use crypto because that is what CryptoBolt covers, but the ideas (orders, costs, risk, position sizing, psychology) apply to any market.

        How long does it take?

        About 2.6 hours of reading in total, plus the practice exercises. Then a 30-day paper trading plan to put it into action. You can go at your own pace.

        Is this financial advice?

        No. It is educational content. Trading carries a high risk of loss, so only risk money you can afford to lose, and speak to a licensed professional about your own situation.
