Trust & transparency

Editorial policy

How CryptoBolt writes its guides, builds and tests its calculators, uses data, takes advertising and fixes mistakes.

Who writes CryptoBolt

CryptoBolt is an independent, self-funded project built and maintained by one person, its founder. There is no newsroom and no paid team, so every page is written, calculated and checked by the same person, and changes are made directly when something is found to be wrong. The founder is not a licensed financial adviser, and nothing on the site is financial, investment, legal or tax advice. See the About page for more.

What we publish

  • Free calculators for planning crypto trades and holdings: liquidation price, position size, futures profit, funding fees, DCA, average price, risk-reward, staking rewards, impermanent loss, spot ROI and Fibonacci levels.
  • Guides and explainers on how crypto markets work, in the blog and the glossary.
  • A live market terminal and a paper-trading simulator using public market data.

How our calculators are built and checked

  • The formula is on the page. Each calculator explains the arithmetic it uses, so you can reproduce the result by hand or in a spreadsheet.
  • Each page includes worked examples with the inputs and outputs written out, and the calculator is pre-filled with one of them.
  • Automated tests compare every calculator against hand-computed values, including edge cases such as a stop-loss on the wrong side of the entry or a price change below −100%.
  • Inputs stay in your browser. The calculators do arithmetic locally and do not send what you type anywhere.
  • Limits are stated. Where a calculator simplifies reality (for example, ignoring exchange-specific maintenance margin or assuming a straight-line price path), the page says so.

How our articles are written

  • We explain mechanics (how a funding rate, a stop-loss or a liquidity pool works) rather than predict prices. We do not publish price targets or tell readers what to buy or sell.
  • Numbers in examples are calculated, not estimated, and are re-checked when a page is updated. Examples are labelled as illustrations, not historical results.
  • Claims about how an exchange or protocol works are based on its public documentation. Rules and fees change, so pages tell you to confirm the current terms with your exchange.
  • Pages show the date they were last reviewed. When we find an error we correct it and, for material changes, record it in the changelog.
  • Some drafting and coding uses AI assistance. Every figure and formula is verified by calculation and testing, and the pages are reviewed by a person before they are published.

Data sources

Live prices, funding rates and open interest come from Binance public market-data endpoints, and supplementary market data comes from CoinGecko. Data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong, so check anything that matters at the source before acting on it. The AI research feature is described on the grounded data page.

Independence and advertising

CryptoBolt shows Google AdSense advertising on some content pages to cover running costs. Advertisers do not influence what is written, and we do not accept payment to review, rank or recommend a coin, exchange or product. Where a page links to a third-party service, the link is there because it is relevant to the topic, and any commercial relationship is stated on that page.

Risk warning

Trading crypto, and especially trading with leverage, can lose you more than you expect, quickly. Staking and providing liquidity carry their own risks, including lock-ups, smart-contract failure and price falls. Calculator results are estimates for planning and education. Past performance does not predict future results. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose, and consider speaking to a qualified adviser about your situation. The terms of service and privacy policy apply to your use of the site.

Corrections and contact

If you find a mistake, an outdated figure or a calculation that does not match your exchange, please tell us at info@cryptobolt.io or through the contact page. We read every message and fix confirmed errors as quickly as we can.
