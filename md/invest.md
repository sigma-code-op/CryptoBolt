## Buying crypto with fiat: what to know

This page is where you buy crypto with fiat — card or bank transfer — through Binance's on-ramp, without leaving the CryptoBolt terminal.

### Does CryptoBolt hold my money or crypto?

No. Orders route directly to Binance, so funds move between your card/bank and your own Binance wallet — CryptoBolt never touches them.

### Do I need to verify my identity?

Yes, through Binance rather than CryptoBolt. Binance's standard KYC and card/bank verification applies before a purchase completes.

### Are there fees?

CryptoBolt adds no markup. Any fees — network, card processing, spread — come from Binance itself, same as trading there directly.

### Can I practice first?

Yes. [Paper trading](https://cryptobolt.io/trade) runs the same buy/sell flow with $10,000 in virtual funds and live prices, zero risk.

### Which coins can I buy with fiat here?

Bitcoin, Ethereum, and the other major pairs Binance lists on its fiat on-ramp — the same card/bank funding options carry over to whichever asset you pick.
