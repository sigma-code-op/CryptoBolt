## What makes these different

**Journal analyzer.** Most trade journals need an account and your exchange keys. This one reads a pasted list, shows expectancy, profit factor, payoff ratio, max drawdown, streaks and fee drag, and tells you the win rate you needed to break even. Nothing leaves your browser.

**Challenge odds.** Funded challenges fail mostly on the daily loss limit, not the profit target. The simulator splits failures into daily-limit, drawdown and timeout so you see which rule actually kills you, and how changing risk per trade moves the answer.

**Safe leverage.** Leverage calculators show your liquidation price. This reverses the question: given your stop, what is the most leverage that keeps liquidation beyond it, with fees and slippage included?

**Slippage.** Spread and fees are advertised; the cost of walking the book is not. Test your real order size on thin altcoins before you trade them.

**Scale-in ladder.** Averaging into a position makes the loss at your stop larger than you think. The ladder sizes each order so the total loss stays at the number you chose.
