## A sensible order to use them

1. **Position size** — decide the amount you are willing to lose, then let the stop-loss distance set the size.
2. **Liquidation price** — confirm your stop is hit well before the exchange would liquidate the position.
3. **Profit calculator** — check the reward against the risk after fees.
4. **Funding fee** — add the carrying cost if you plan to hold longer than a day or two.

Every calculator is an estimate for planning, not a prediction or advice. To test a plan with no real money at stake, run it in the free [crypto paper trading simulator](https://cryptobolt.io/crypto-paper-trading.html) or watch live prices and funding in the [trading terminal](https://cryptobolt.io/app.html).

FAQ

## Free crypto trading calculators FAQ

### Are these calculators free?

Yes. They run in your browser, need no signup, and do not send your inputs anywhere.

### Are the results exact?

No. They are estimates using standard formulas. Exchanges add fees, tiered maintenance margin and funding, so check your exchange for the exact figure on a live position.

### Which calculator should I use first?

Start with position size: decide how much you are willing to lose, then check the liquidation price and fees for that position.
