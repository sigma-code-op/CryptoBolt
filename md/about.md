# About CryptoBolt | Crypto Terminal & AI Research

Source: https://cryptobolt.io/about.html

## What it is, and isn't

CryptoBolt tracks markets and helps you think about them. It doesn't touch your money.

### What CryptoBolt is

- Live market data sourced from Binance and CoinGecko public APIs
- AI-generated market commentary (technical + sentiment) via Groq, using your own API key
- A paper trading simulator with virtual funds — for practice only
- A portfolio tracker for coins you already hold elsewhere

### What CryptoBolt is not

- Not a cryptocurrency exchange, broker, or custodian
- Does not hold, transfer, or have access to your funds
- Does not place real trades — paper trading uses simulated balances only
- Not a source of financial advice

Buy/sell of real crypto is handled entirely by [Binance](https://www.binance.com), a separate third-party exchange — CryptoBolt never touches that transaction.

## Why it exists

Most free crypto terminals are cluttered or paywalled. CryptoBolt started as a personal project to have one clean, fast screen with live prices, charts, and an AI second opinion — and grew into something worth sharing publicly.

## How the AI research works

The AI market read isn't a black box guessing at numbers. It's grounded in live inputs — current price action, funding rates, and the Fear & Greed Index — and run through your own Groq API key rather than a shared, rate-limited backend. That means the commentary reflects what the market is actually doing right now, not a stale training snapshot. See [how the pipeline stays grounded](https://cryptobolt.io/ai-research-grounded-data.html) for the technical breakdown.

## Where the data comes from

Prices and order flow are pulled from Binance's public market-data API; supplementary market data comes from CoinGecko. Nothing is proxied through a paid data vendor, which is part of why the terminal stays free to use.
