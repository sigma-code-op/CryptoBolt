## More under the hood.

### Market screener & backtester

Build a rule from RSI, MACD, EMA/SMA, Bollinger Bands, VWAP, ATR and more, then scan the live market for matches or backtest it against months of history — win rate, avg return, and an equity curve.

### Paper trading account

Buy and sell at live prices with virtual funds. Track holdings, P&L, and an equity curve to test a strategy before risking real money.

### Fiat on/off-ramp

Buy or sell crypto directly with a card or bank transfer through a secure, signed widget flow — no separate exchange account needed.

### Cross-device sync

Sign in and your watchlist, alerts, and portfolio follow you — synced in the background with automatic conflict resolution.

### Global market pulse

Total market cap, 24h volume, BTC dominance, and the Fear & Greed index at a glance, sourced from CoinGecko and Alternative.me.

### Position size & risk calculator

Work out position size, risk-per-trade, and reward ratios before you place an order — deterministic math, not an AI guess. Pair it with the standalone [position size calculator](https://cryptobolt.io/position-size-calculator) and [liquidation price calculator](https://cryptobolt.io/liquidation-price-calculator) to check where a leveraged position would actually get closed out.

### Free trading calculators

Four browser-based tools that need no signup: [liquidation price](https://cryptobolt.io/liquidation-price-calculator), [position size](https://cryptobolt.io/position-size-calculator), [futures profit & ROE](https://cryptobolt.io/crypto-profit-calculator) and [funding fees](https://cryptobolt.io/funding-fee-calculator). Plan a leveraged trade before you risk real money, or browse them all on the [calculators page](https://cryptobolt.io/crypto-calculators).

### Top movers & trending

A live scrolling strip of the day's biggest movers and trending coins, so you catch what's moving without hunting for it.
