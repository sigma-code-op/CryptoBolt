# How paper trading works

Practice with $10,000 of virtual money before risking real money.

## What you will learn

- How a paper account starts with $10,000 of virtual funds
- Why to set the stop-loss and take-profit before entering
- What paper trading cannot teach you: emotion, fees and slippage

Read the transcript

Step one: every account starts with $10,000 in virtual funds. Step two: pick a market and trade at live prices. Step three: set your size, stop-loss and take-profit, so the exit is planned before the entry. Step four: review your results and learn from each trade. In the example, a closed trade shows a gain of $21.25 and the balance updates. You can reset the balance any time to test a new idea from a clean slate. Remember: paper results are practice, not a promise, test your plan, stops and sizing first, and real markets add emotion, fees and slippage. The numbers are illustrative and this is educational content, not financial advice.

## A worked example

Start with $10,000 of virtual funds and a 1% risk limit, so $100 per trade. You plan a long on BTC at $50,000 with a stop at $49,000 (2% away) and a take-profit at $52,000 (4% away).

| Item | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Risk per trade | $100 |
| Position size | $5,000 (0.1 BTC) |
| Loss if stop is hit | $100 |
| Gain if target is hit | $200 |
| Reward-to-risk | 2 to 1 |

The prices are made up to show the method. The point of practice is to repeat a plan like this many times and see how it behaves, not to chase one lucky trade.

## Common mistakes

- Using far bigger sizes than you would with real money. Practice habits carry over.
- Skipping the stop-loss because the money is not real.
- Judging a strategy after three or four trades. A small sample says very little.
- Assuming paper results will match real results. Emotion, fees and slippage change the outcome.

## Frequently asked questions

What is paper trading?

Paper trading means practicing with virtual money at live market prices, so you can test ideas and learn how an exchange works without risking real funds.

Is paper trading free?

Yes. CryptoBolt starts every paper account with $10,000 of virtual funds, and you can reset the balance whenever you want.

How long should I paper trade before using real money?

There is no fixed number. A useful target is enough trades to see your results across different market conditions, with a written plan you follow consistently.

Can paper trading predict real results?

No. It is a practice tool. Real trading adds emotion, fees and slippage that a simulation may not fully capture.
