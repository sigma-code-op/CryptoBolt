FAQ

## Common questions about CryptoBolt

### Is CryptoBolt free to use?

Yes. CryptoBolt is free — live prices, charts, order book, portfolio tracking, and alerts all run with no signup. The AI market analysis feature uses a bring-your-own-key model with Groq, so you can add your own free Groq API key to enable it.

### Where does CryptoBolt get its live crypto price data?

Prices, order book depth, and candlestick data stream directly from the Binance public spot and futures WebSocket and REST APIs, so quotes update in real time.

### How does the crypto AI market analysis work?

CryptoBolt's crypto AI combines multi-timeframe technical confluence, ATR volatility, volume trends, funding-rate positioning, live news, and the Fear & Greed index into a structured market read, rather than relying on a single indicator.

### Can I practice trading before using real money?

Yes. The paper trading account lets you buy and sell at live prices with virtual funds, and tracks holdings, P&L, and an equity curve so you can test a strategy risk-free.

### Does CryptoBolt support both spot and futures tracking?

Yes. CryptoBolt tracks spot portfolio holdings alongside leveraged futures positions, including an estimated liquidation price, stop-loss/take-profit risk-reward, and unrealized P&L.
