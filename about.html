About CryptoBolt

CryptoBolt is an independent, self-funded crypto market terminal — live spot & futures prices, charting, an AI-generated market read, and a risk-free paper trading simulator. It's built and maintained by a single developer, not a company or exchange.

What CryptoBolt is

  • Live market data sourced from Binance and CoinGecko public APIs
  • AI-generated market commentary (technical + sentiment) via Groq, using your own API key
  • A paper trading simulator with virtual funds — for practice only
  • A portfolio tracker for coins you already hold elsewhere

What CryptoBolt is not

  • It is not a cryptocurrency exchange, broker, or custodian
  • It does not hold, transfer, or have access to your funds
  • It does not place real trades — paper trading uses simulated balances only
  • Buy/sell of real crypto is handled entirely by Transak, a separately regulated third-party on-ramp — CryptoBolt never touches that transaction
  • Nothing on this site is financial advice

Why it exists

Most free crypto terminals are cluttered or paywalled. CryptoBolt started as a personal project to have one clean, fast screen with live prices, charts, and an AI second opinion — and grew into something worth sharing publicly.
