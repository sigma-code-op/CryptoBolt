Who we are

One person, one screen, no paywall.

CryptoBolt (also written Crypto Bolt) is an independent, self-funded crypto market terminal — live spot & futures prices, charting, an AI-generated market read, and a risk-free paper trading simulator. It's built and maintained by one person, not a company or exchange.

What it is, and isn't

CryptoBolt tracks markets and helps you think about them. It doesn't touch your money.

What CryptoBolt is

  • Live market data sourced from Binance and CoinGecko public APIs
  • AI-generated market commentary (technical + sentiment) via Groq, using your own API key
  • A paper trading simulator with virtual funds — for practice only
  • A portfolio tracker for coins you already hold elsewhere

What CryptoBolt is not

  • Not a cryptocurrency exchange, broker, or custodian
  • Does not hold, transfer, or have access to your funds
  • Does not place real trades — paper trading uses simulated balances only
  • Not a source of financial advice

Buy/sell of real crypto is handled entirely by Binance, a separate third-party exchange — CryptoBolt never touches that transaction.

Why it exists

Most free crypto terminals are cluttered or paywalled. CryptoBolt started as a personal project to have one clean, fast screen with live prices, charts, and an AI second opinion — and grew into something worth sharing publicly.

How the AI research works

The AI market read isn't a black box guessing at numbers. It's grounded in live inputs — current price action, funding rates, and the Fear & Greed Index — and run through your own Groq API key rather than a shared, rate-limited backend. That means the commentary reflects what the market is actually doing right now, not a stale training snapshot. See how the pipeline stays grounded for the technical breakdown.

Where the data comes from

Prices and order flow are pulled from Binance's public market-data API; supplementary market data comes from CoinGecko. Nothing is proxied through a paid data vendor, which is part of why the terminal stays free to use.

Roadmap

Development is ongoing and mostly driven by user feedback submitted through the contact form. Recent additions include a paper trading leaderboard, portfolio tracking, and price alerts — see the features page for the current list.
