Who it's for

Beginners, strategy testers, and anyone switching markets.

New traders use paper trading to learn order types and chart reading without a real balance on the line. More experienced traders use it to backtest an idea — a breakout setup, a funding-rate play, a new risk model — against live conditions before sizing it with real capital. It's also useful if you're moving from spot to futures for the first time: leverage and liquidation behave very differently, and a $10,000 virtual account is a safer place to feel that out than a live exchange.

FAQ

Crypto paper trading basics

Is paper trading free?

Yes. CryptoBolt includes a free virtual account for practice trading with live market conditions.

Does it use real markets?

Yes. It runs with live market prices, so you can practice in a realistic environment without using real money.

Can I switch to real trading later?

Yes. Once you're ready, the dedicated Invest page is separate from the virtual paper trading account so the risk model stays clear.

How much virtual money do I start with?

Every account starts with $10,000 in virtual funds, and you can reset your balance any time to test a strategy from a clean slate.

Can I practice futures, not just spot?

Yes. The simulator supports simulated spot and futures positions, so you can practice leverage and funding-rate mechanics risk-free.
