    How to calculate crypto profit on any coin

    The arithmetic is the same for every coin; only the price changes. For a spot trade in dollars:

    • Buy fee = amount invested × fee %
    • Coins bought = (amount invested − buy fee) ÷ buy price
    • Sale proceeds = coins bought × sell price
    • Sell fee = sale proceeds × fee %
    • Net profit = sale proceeds − sell fee − amount invested
    • ROI = net profit ÷ amount invested × 100

    Worked example

    Suppose you put $1,000 into a coin at an illustrative price of $1.50 and sell at $2.40, paying a 0.1% fee on each side. These prices are made up for the example, not a quote for any real coin:

    • Buy fee: $1.00, so $999.00 buys 666 coins
    • Proceeds: 666 × $2.40 = $1,598.40, less a $1.60 sell fee = $1,596.80
    • Net profit: $1,596.80 − $1,000 = $596.80, an ROI of 59.68% on a 60% price move
    • Break-even sell price: about $1.503, because fees on both sides must be earned back first

    Before you trust a profit number

    • Liquidity and spread. On small-cap coins the price you actually get can differ from the chart, especially on larger orders.
    • Token unlocks and supply. Scheduled releases of locked tokens can add selling pressure that a simple profit target ignores.
    • Taxes. Gains are often taxable; the calculator shows pre-tax profit.
    • Position size. Decide how much you can lose first with the position size calculator.

    FAQ

    Crypto profit calculator FAQ

    How do I calculate profit on FET, NEAR, ANKR or any other coin?

    Subtract the buy fee from your investment and divide by the buy price to get the number of coins. Multiply the coins by your sell price, subtract the sell fee, then subtract what you invested. The result is your net profit; divide by the amount invested for ROI.

    What is the break-even price of a crypto trade?

    It is the sell price at which you get back exactly what you invested after paying fees on both the buy and the sell. It is always slightly above your buy price: about 0.2% higher at a 0.1% fee per side.

    Does this calculator include taxes?

    No. Capital gains tax rules depend on your country and how long you held the coin. Treat the result as a pre-tax figure and check your local rules.

    Which fee should I enter?

    Use the rate your exchange charges for the order type you will use. Spot taker fees on large exchanges are often a tenth of a percent per side or lower, but card purchases, brokers and small exchanges can charge far more.

    Is this for futures or leveraged trades?

    No, it covers spot buys and sells. For leverage, margin and ROE use the crypto futures profit calculator, and check the liquidation price before you open the trade.
