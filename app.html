A free, real-time crypto tracker & trading terminal

CryptoBolt is a free live crypto price tracker and trading terminal built for people who want more than a basic price ticker. Live Bitcoin, Ethereum, and altcoin prices stream straight from Binance's spot and futures WebSocket feeds, so every quote, candle, and order book update reflects the current crypto market — not a delayed snapshot.

Beyond raw prices, CryptoBolt pairs TradingView-grade charting (RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, EMA/MA, VWAP) with an AI-powered crypto market analysis panel. The AI reads live news, Fear & Greed sentiment, multi-timeframe confluence, and volatility together, so you get a reasoned market read instead of a single indicator's opinion.

Frequently asked questions

Is CryptoBolt free to use?

Yes. Live prices, charts, order book, portfolio tracking, and alerts all run with no signup. The AI market analysis feature uses a bring-your-own-key model with Groq, so you add your own free Groq API key to enable it.

Where does CryptoBolt get its live crypto price data?

Prices, order book depth, and candlestick data stream directly from the Binance public spot and futures APIs, so quotes update in real time.

How does the AI crypto market analysis work?

It combines multi-timeframe technical confluence, ATR volatility, volume trends, funding-rate positioning, live news, and the Fear & Greed index into a structured AI-generated market read, rather than relying on a single indicator.

Can I practice trading before using real money?

Yes — the paper trading account lets you buy and sell at live prices with virtual funds, and tracks holdings, P&L, and an equity curve so you can test a strategy risk-free.

Does CryptoBolt support both spot and futures tracking?

Yes. CryptoBolt tracks spot portfolio holdings alongside leveraged futures positions, including an estimated liquidation price, stop-loss/take-profit risk-reward, and unrealized P&L.
